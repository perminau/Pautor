Pautor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Pautor is a photo clean-up app. It is built to work entirely on your device, and it does not collect, store or share your personal data.

What Pautor does with your photos

Pautor asks for access to your photo library so it can show you photos taken on a given day and find similar ones. All of this analysis happens on your device. Your photos, and anything derived from them, never leave your device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

When you choose to delete photos, Pautor asks iOS to move them to your Recently Deleted album, where you can restore them for 30 days.

What Pautor stores

  • On your device: which days you have reviewed, your in-progress selections, simple statistics (such as the number of photos removed), and your settings.
  • In your iCloud account (via Apple's iCloud key-value storage): whether you have unlocked the app, which days you have reviewed, and your summary statistics, so that your progress and unlock carry over if you reinstall or change devices. This data lives in your own iCloud account and is not accessible to us.

What Pautor does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No data is sent to our servers. We do not operate any servers for this app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment information.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Pautor schedules a local notification on your device. It is not sent from a server.

Children

Pautor does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
