Frequently asked questions

How does Pautor work?

Pick a day from your calendar. Pautor groups photos that look alike, you mark the ones you don't want, and when you confirm, they are moved to your Recently Deleted album. Tap "Mark day reviewed" when you're done with a day.

Did I permanently delete a photo by mistake?

Not yet. Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can restore them for 30 days.

Why can't I see some of my photos, or why is it asking for photo access?

Pautor needs access to your photo library. Open Settings → Pautor → Photos and choose Full Access (or select the photos you want it to see).

A photo says "Loading from iCloud…"

If you use iCloud Photos with "Optimize Storage," the full-size photo may need to download first. It appears once the download finishes. Make sure you have a connection.

How much is free, and what does the purchase include?

You can review your first 30 days for free. A single one-time purchase then unlocks unlimited days forever. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Pautor, tap the gear icon on the home screen, then Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

I reinstalled or got a new phone. Is my progress gone?

Your unlock, reviewed days and statistics are backed up to your iCloud account and should return automatically. This requires that you're signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled for the device. It can take a minute after the first launch.

Does the app send my photos anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn on the daily reminder?

Gear icon → Reminders. Switch it on and choose a time. If notifications are blocked, enable them in Settings → Notifications → Pautor.

I have a promo code. Where do I enter it?

On the paywall screen, tap the promo code option and enter the code exactly as given.
